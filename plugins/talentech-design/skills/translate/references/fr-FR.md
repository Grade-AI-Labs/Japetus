# Talentech - French

Use natural contemporary French for France (`fr-FR`).

- Address users consistently with `vous`.
- Keep the language professional, clear, friendly, and respectful without becoming bureaucratic.
- Follow natural French grammar and word order rather than English structure.
- Use correct contractions, gender, number, articles, and agreement.
- Prefer concise infinitive UI actions when conventional, such as `Enregistrer`, `Créer`, `Modifier`, `Supprimer`, `Télécharger`.
- Use sentence case and normal French punctuation, including French spacing conventions where supported.
- Use French date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer established French terminology over unnecessary anglicisms.
- Use inclusive wording naturally; do not introduce midpoint forms or other special inclusive typography unless approved.
- Inflect approved glossary terms naturally.

Avoid literal English word order, unnecessary anglicisms, bureaucratic phrasing, inconsistent terminology, and overuse of nominal constructions.
