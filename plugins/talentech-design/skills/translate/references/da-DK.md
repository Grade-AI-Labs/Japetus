# Talentech - Danish

Use natural contemporary Danish for Denmark (`da-DK`).

- Address the user with `du` when direct address is needed.
- Keep wording clear, direct, friendly, and professional.
- Follow natural Danish word order rather than English syntax.
- Prefer concise verb-led UI actions, such as `Gem`, `Opret`, `Rediger`, `Slet`, `Download`.
- Avoid unnecessary passive constructions, formal phrasing, and noun-heavy language.
- Use sentence case and Danish punctuation conventions.
- Use Danish date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer established Danish product terminology over unnecessary English loanwords.
- Use inclusive wording naturally and avoid unnecessary gender assumptions.
- Adapt approved glossary terms naturally for grammar and compounds.

Avoid English sentence structure, bureaucratic language, inconsistent terminology, and unnecessary anglicisms.
