# Talentech - German

Use natural, contemporary German for Germany (`de-DE`).

- Address users consistently with **Sie**, including `Ihnen` and `Ihr`.
- Keep the language professional and direct without becoming bureaucratic or ceremonial.
- Follow natural German sentence structure rather than English word order.
- Use correct case, gender, number, articles and agreement.
- Restructure English noun chains when needed.
- Use German compounds naturally, but avoid unnecessarily long or difficult compounds.
- Prefer conventional infinitive UI actions, e.g. `Speichern`, `Erstellen`, `Bearbeiten`, `Löschen`, `Herunterladen`.
- Follow German capitalization rules, especially noun capitalization. Do not copy English title case.
- Use German conventions for numbers, dates, time and currency when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer neutral wording where natural. Do not introduce gender stars, colons, underscores or similar forms unless explicitly approved.
- Adapt glossary terms naturally for case and number.

Avoid unnecessary anglicisms, excessive nominalization, inconsistent `Sie`, overly long compounds and English sentence structure.
