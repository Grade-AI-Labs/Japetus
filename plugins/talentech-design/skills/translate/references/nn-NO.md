# Talentech - Norwegian Nynorsk

Use natural contemporary Norwegian Nynorsk for Norway (`nn-NO`).

- Use genuine Nynorsk forms rather than Bokmal with isolated spelling substitutions.
- Address the user with `du` when direct address is needed.
- Keep the language clear, direct, friendly, and professional.
- Follow natural Nynorsk syntax and morphology.
- Prefer concise verb-led UI actions when natural.
- Use sentence case and Norwegian punctuation conventions.
- Use Norwegian date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer established Nynorsk terminology and avoid unnecessary English loanwords.
- Use inclusive wording naturally.
- Adapt approved glossary terms naturally for case, number, compounds, and grammatical context.

Avoid drifting into Bokmal, literal English syntax, overly formal wording, and inconsistent Nynorsk forms.
