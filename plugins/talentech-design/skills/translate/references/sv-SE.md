# Talentech - Swedish

Use natural contemporary Swedish for Sweden (`sv-SE`).

- Address the user with `du` when direct address is needed.
- Keep wording clear, direct, friendly, and professional.
- Follow natural Swedish syntax rather than English word order.
- Prefer concise verb-led UI actions, such as `Spara`, `Skapa`, `Redigera`, `Ta bort`, `Ladda ner`.
- Avoid unnecessary passive constructions and heavy noun phrases.
- Use sentence case and Swedish punctuation conventions.
- Use Swedish date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer established Swedish terminology over unnecessary English words.
- Use inclusive wording naturally and avoid unnecessary gender assumptions.
- Inflect and compound approved glossary terms naturally.

Avoid English sentence structure, unnecessary formality, inconsistent terminology, and direct calques that sound unnatural in Swedish.
