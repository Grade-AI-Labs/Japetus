# Talentech - Norwegian Bokmal

Use natural contemporary Norwegian Bokmal for Norway (`nb-NO`).

- Address the user with `du` when direct address is needed.
- Keep the language clear, direct, friendly, and professional.
- Follow natural Norwegian syntax rather than English word order.
- Prefer concise verb-led UI actions, such as `Lagre`, `Opprett`, `Rediger`, `Slett`, `Last ned`.
- Avoid unnecessary passive constructions and overly formal wording.
- Use sentence case and Norwegian punctuation conventions.
- Use Norwegian date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Prefer established Norwegian terminology over unnecessary English loanwords.
- Use inclusive wording naturally and avoid unnecessary gender assumptions.
- Adapt approved glossary terms naturally for grammar, compounds, and number.

Avoid English word order, bureaucratic language, unnecessary anglicisms, and inconsistent Bokmal terminology.
