# Talentech - Dutch

Use natural, contemporary Dutch for the Netherlands (`nl-NL`).

- Address users with **je**.
- Use `je` and `jouw` consistently where direct address is needed.
- Do not switch to formal `u` unless the context specifically requires it.
- Keep the language direct, friendly and professional.
- Follow natural Dutch word order rather than English syntax.
- Avoid literal translations of English noun chains and unnecessary passive constructions.
- Prefer concise infinitive UI actions, e.g. `Opslaan`, `Maken`, `Bewerken`, `Verwijderen`, `Downloaden`.
- Use sentence case and normal Dutch punctuation.
- Follow Dutch compound-word rules. Do not split compounds simply because English uses separate words.
- Use Dutch conventions for numbers, dates, time and currency when formatting belongs in the content.
- Use inclusive wording naturally and avoid unnecessary gender assumptions.
- Use approved Dutch terminology consistently and avoid unnecessary English loanwords.

Avoid English word order, separated compounds, unnecessary anglicisms and inconsistent switching between `je` and `u`.
