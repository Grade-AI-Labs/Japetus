# Talentech - Finnish

Use natural contemporary Finnish for Finland (`fi-FI`).

- Prefer clear, direct Finnish over literal English structures.
- Avoid unnecessary personal pronouns; Finnish often expresses the subject through the verb or context.
- Use concise imperative forms for actions when natural, such as `Tallenna`, `Luo`, `Muokkaa`, `Poista`, `Lataa`.
- Adapt case, number, possessive structure, and verb form naturally around variables.
- Avoid long noun chains when a clearer phrase is possible.
- Prefer established Finnish product terminology over unnecessary English loanwords.
- Use sentence case and Finnish punctuation conventions.
- Use Finnish date, number, time, and currency conventions when formatting belongs in the content.
- Use gender-neutral wording naturally; Finnish usually allows this without special forms.
- Inflect approved glossary terms naturally instead of forcing dictionary forms.

Avoid translated-English syntax, unnecessary pronouns, excessive nominalization, and technical anglicisms when a clear Finnish term exists.
