# Talentech product context

Use this to sharpen questions and to recognise constraints the user has not named. It is context, not evidence: a claim about a customer, a market or a law still needs its source.

## Products

Talentech's HR-tech and ATS products: **Reachmee**, **Talentrecruiter**, **Webcruiter** and **Varbi**. Ask which product or products the idea touches; they have different architectures, customers and markets. Ask whether the idea belongs in one product or across several.

## Markets

The Nordics (Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland) and Benelux. Ask which markets, whether public or private sector, and which languages (SV, NO, DA, FI, NL, EN).

## Users and buyers

The person who uses an HR tool is often not the one who buys it: the recruiter or hiring manager uses it, an HR director buys it, and candidates are affected by it without choosing it. Ask who each of these is for the idea.

## Constraints to probe

- **AI features:** Azure OpenAI limits (rate, region, model availability), latency budget and cost per call; what the user sees and does when the model fails or is unavailable.
- **Legal and compliance:** GDPR; the EU AI Act, where AI used in recruitment can be classified high-risk; sector and country rules (Swedish public-sector transparency, Norwegian preferential rights, the Finnish exclusion memo); customer DPAs.
- **Audit and explainability:** can a recruiter defend an AI-influenced decision to a candidate, a regulator or a court?
- **Adversarial input:** prompt injection inside a CV or job ad, candidates gaming the system, biased inputs.
- **Bad data:** empty, garbled, attachment-only or unexpected-language CVs.
- **Organisation:** team capacity and dependencies on other teams.

## Competitors

Teamtailor, EasyCruit and others. Treat what competitors do as input, never as the justification.
