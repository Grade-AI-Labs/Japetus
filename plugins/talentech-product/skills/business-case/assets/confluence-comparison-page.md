One row per case, pasted from the comparison row the skill prints. Reading the table:

- Value 12m is € per year at the 12-month run-rate: the base case for large cases, the band midpoint for small and medium (XS 12k, S 60k, M 300k, L 1.2M, XL 2.5M).
- Confidence is proposed from the evidence ladder (30 / 50 / 70 / 90%); an override is explained in the case's notes.
- Two cases in the same band cannot be separated by this table; the steering group separates them, or asks the PM for a formula.
- Strategic cases show no value; they are ranked on effort and on the cases they unlock, named in the case.
