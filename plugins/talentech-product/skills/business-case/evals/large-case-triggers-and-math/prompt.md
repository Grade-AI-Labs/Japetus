I'm the PM for ReachMee and I want to pitch an AI interview-summary add-on sold per seat to the management team. To save time, here are my answers to everything you'd ask:
- What: AI summaries of structured interview notes, sold as a paid add-on at 5 euro per user per month. Customers today write summaries by hand in the ATS or in Word.
- Products: ReachMee first (Invest & grow), then Varbi (Stay relevant & harvest). It would be a shared service.
- Effort: two backend devs at 80% for 10 weeks, one frontend dev at 50% for 10 weeks, UX at 40% for 6 weeks, me at 30% for 14 weeks, legal review about 20 hours, sales enablement about 40 hours. I estimated it alone.
- Triggers: new pricing (yes), partner contract (no), more than one product (yes), capacity outside the team (no), new sensitive data (no, interview notes are already in the ATS).
- Value: expansion ARR. 1,200 recruiter seats across ReachMee; I assume 20% take it in year one and 45% by year three at 60 euro per seat per year. Low case 10% and 30%, high case 30% and 60%.
- Evidence: 14 Productboard votes and usage data showing 60% of interviews get a written summary. No customer interviews yet.
- Yearly cost after launch: LLM API cost about 9,000 euro per year at year-one volume, support maybe 60 hours.
- Risks: quality of summaries in Norwegian and Finnish; procurement in public sector might object to AI; dependency on the AI platform team's gateway. Kill: if fewer than 5 of 10 beta customers rate summaries usable by week 8 after launch.
- Why now: competitors have shipped similar features; two tenders this spring asked for AI summaries.
- Success metric: paid seats at 12 months, target 240; review 12 months after launch; owner me.
- I'd say confidence 80%.
Write the case and tell me what you'd still challenge.
